Why 4 and 92: the evidence behind the age range

Age bounds: why 4 and 92

Age is kept only between 4 and 92; any other value becomes Unknown. No user is removed, only the age value. Age is used only for the churn-by-age-group cut. All counts are from users.csv (1,691,575 users); every % is of all users.

Lower bound: 4

Recommendation
Keep ages 4 and above. Set ages 1-3 to Unknown (37 users, 0.002% of all users).

Why: Ages 1-3 look like users who never filled in their profile; ages 4-7 look like ages 8-12.

Caveat: The empty-profile signal comes mostly from age 1 (7 of 19 users). For ages 2-3 the evidence is the jump in counts. Only 37 users are affected, so this choice has almost no effect on results.

Upper bound: 92

Recommendation
Keep ages up to 92. Set ages 93+ to Unknown (1,850 users, 0.109% of all users).

Why: In any real population there are fewer people at each older age. Here the count stops falling at 92 and rises again.

Caveat: Behaviour does not separate 81-92 from 93+: gender missing, city, acquisition channel, registration year and paid share are all similar. The spike ages do not share a registration date. The evidence is the shape of the counts only.
